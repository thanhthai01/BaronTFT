Champion tile — portrait, cost coin, traits, and a tier-colored frame (border/glow follow the cost). The core unit of a roster, tier list, or comp view.

```jsx
<ChampionCard name="Ahri" cost={4} traits={['Pháp Sư', 'Học Giả']} />
<ChampionCard name="Garen" cost={1} traits={['Đấu Sĩ']} portrait="/img/garen.png" />
```

Provide `portrait` for real art; without it a tinted initial stands in.
