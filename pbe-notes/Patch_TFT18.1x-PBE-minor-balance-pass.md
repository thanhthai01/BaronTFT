## Balance

### Champions

#### 3 Cost

- ▼ **Krug**
  - Spell Damage %HP Damage: 10% → 8%

#### 5 Cost

- ▼ **Gnar**
  - Leap Damage: 150/225 AD → 100/150 AD

#### 3-star 4-costs

- ▲ **Amumu**
  - 3* Max HP Heal: 2.2% → 4%
- ▲ **Lillia**
  - 3* Heal: 600 AP → 800 AP
- ▲ **Nidalee (AD)**
  - 3* Armor Ignore: 40% → 80%

### Tộc Hệ

- ▼ **Blossom**
  - Gold Per Charm Purchased: 5 → 4
- **Coven**
  - 500 Essence Cashout with 4 random emblems removed.

### Linh Hỏa (Wisps)

- **Nature's Wrath**
  - Re-enabled
  - Better tailoring for when it is offered
  - Cost: 6g → 1g
  - Delay: 8s → 10s
  - Upgrade Delay: 6s → 8s
